Inventory defaults

Pre-filled when a new ingredient is created. Each ingredient keeps its own window.

Enter a whole number from 1 to 365.

Used by Demand Estimation and the purchase list.

Enter a number from 0 to 14.

Usable stock at or below this share of the reorder level shows Critical.

Enter a whole number from 1 to 100.

Usable stock at or above this many times the reorder level shows a green stock bar. The bar is drawn as stock ÷ (multiple × reorder level), so green always means a full bar.

Enter a number from 1.5 to 5.

These feed the formulas in the README: Purchase Quantity = Forecast Demand + Safety Stock − Usable Stock − Incoming Stock (never below zero, safety stock = 1 day of forecast usage), and Critical = usable stock ≤ 50% of the reorder level.
